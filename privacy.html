Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how the 11 Titolari upload tool (the "App") handles data when it connects to TikTok.

Data we access

Only data of the TikTok account owned by 11 Titolari: basic profile information (display name) and the permission to upload videos as drafts. We do not access data of other TikTok users.

How we use it

The data is used only to upload our own videos to our own account and to check the status of each upload.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Contact

For questions, contact us through our YouTube channel.
