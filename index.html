11 TITOLARI

11 Titolari is an Italian football channel. We make short documentaries about the careers of players and coaches, published on YouTube, with short clips on TikTok and Instagram.

Our upload tool

We use a small internal tool that sends our own short videos to our own TikTok account as drafts. We then review and publish them ourselves in the TikTok app. The tool is not offered to the public.
